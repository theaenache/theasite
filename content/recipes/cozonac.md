## Ingredients

- 500g flour
- 200ml warm milk
- 3 eggs
- 150g sugar
- 100g butter
- 25g fresh yeast
- pinch of salt
- zest of 1 lemon

### For the filling
- 200g walnuts, ground
- 4 tbsp cocoa powder
- 100g sugar
- 2 egg whites

## Instructions

1. Dissolve the yeast in warm milk with a pinch of sugar. Let it rest for 10 minutes.
2. Mix flour, sugar, salt, and lemon zest. Add eggs, milk mixture, and butter. Knead until smooth.
3. Let the dough rise for 1 hour.
4. Make the filling: mix walnuts, cocoa, sugar, and egg whites.
5. Roll out the dough, spread the filling, roll it up, and braid.
6. Let it rise again for 30 minutes. Bake at 180°C for 45 minutes.
